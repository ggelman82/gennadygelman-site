Antidepressant Chooser

Clinical-opinion disclaimer

These recommendations reflect my clinical opinions. They are not a guideline, diagnosis, or substitute for shared decision-making, current prescribing information, medication reconciliation, or independent clinician judgment.

Treatment history

Was there a clear past benefit?

Consider the patient and first-degree relatives. A convincing prior response can be more useful than small average differences between medications.

Pathway logic

  1. Prior or family benefit
  2. Safety screen
  3. Pain co-target
  4. Anxiety and activation
  5. Side-effect priorities
  6. Interactions and age

A failed patient trial deliberately falls back into the tree. The same is true when duloxetine has already failed in the pain branch.
