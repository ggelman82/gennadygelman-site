Calculation reference guide and sources

Selected primary or authoritative references supporting the formulas and interpretive guardrails used in this tool. Individual result cards include the most relevant source on their reverse side.

  • KDIGO 2024 CKD guideline — eGFR and albuminuria categories
  • Hillier et al. — glucose-adjusted sodium
  • Adjusted calcium limitations review — albumin-adjusted calcium
  • Protein-gap screening study — protein gap and monoclonal gammopathy
  • Mentzer index report — microcytosis screening ratio
  • Original FIB-4 validation — liver-fibrosis risk stratification
  • HRSA/OPTN MELD calculator — MELD 3.0
  • ADA Standards of Care 2026 — A1c and glycemia
  • Original HOMA model — HOMA-IR
  • QUICKI validation — insulin sensitivity
  • TyG validation — triglyceride-glucose index
  • AHA/ACC cholesterol guideline — lipid relationships
  • International PCOS guideline — limits of LH/FSH interpretation
  • Alzheimer’s Association guideline — blood biomarker interpretation
Clinician use only. This tool provides clinical decision support and is not a diagnostic device. It does not know the full history, symptoms, medications, baseline values, timing, local reference ranges, or specimen quality. Verify every parsed value and apply clinical judgment.
