How the illustrative factor is selected

Table 4 lists reference Lp(a) <75 nmol/L; 1.2× at 75–124; 1.4× at 125; 2× at 250; 3× at 350; and 4× at 430 nmol/L. For this illustration, values between the listed anchors use the lower anchor: 125–<250 → 1.4×, 250–<350 → 2×, 350–<430 → 3×, and ≥430 → 4×. These stepwise bands are this tool’s approximation, not exact guideline cutoffs for a continuous biological relationship. Below 75, 1× is a display convention; no downward adjustment is made. Values above 430 are not extrapolated beyond 4×.

Only nmol/L is accepted here; do not enter mg/dL or use a fixed unit conversion. A chart value reported as <X or >X is handled by the existing parser as X; confirm the actual result before interpreting a factor.

Source: 2026 ACC/AHA multisociety dyslipidemia guideline, Table 4.

Guidelines & supporting evidenceRead more: evidence behind this tool

These are the principal guidelines, derivation studies, and validation studies supporting the calculations and decision pathways used above. Links open the original publication or official project page.

Current guidelines

2026 ACC/AHA Multisociety Dyslipidemia Guideline

Published March 13, 2026. Introduces PREVENT-based treatment thresholds, LDL and non-HDL goals, risk enhancers, and selective CAC reclassification.

2025 AHA/ACC High Blood Pressure Guideline

Supports contemporary BP thresholds, treatment decisions, and integration of predicted cardiovascular risk.

Official AHA PREVENT Calculator

Clinical implementation of 10- and 30-year CVD, ASCVD, and heart-failure risk estimates, including PREVENT-Age.

PREVENT models

Development and Validation of the AHA PREVENT Equations

Khan et al., Circulation, 2024. Derivation and validation of the sex-specific, race-free PREVENT equations in more than 6 million adults.

External Validation of PREVENT

Scheuermann et al., JAMA Network Open, 2024. Evaluates discrimination and calibration in a nationally representative US cohort.

PREVENT Risk Age Equations

Krishnan et al., JAMA Cardiology, 2025. Describes the PREVENT-Age communication metric displayed in this tool.

Coronary calcium & MESA

CAC Added to the PREVENT-ASCVD Equations

Huang et al., JAMA, 2026. Finds that CAC provides its greatest reclassification value in borderline- and intermediate-risk patients.

Derivation and Validation of the MESA CAC Risk Score

McClelland et al., JACC, 2015. Establishes the 10-year CHD model using traditional risk factors with and without CAC.

Official Race-Free MESA Risk Score

MESA’s current race-free calculator and project description. The model excludes race/ethnicity and related interaction terms.
