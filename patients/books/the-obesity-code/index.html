The Obesity Code

by Jason Fung, MD

Jason Fung argues that body weight is regulated by hormones and food timing as well as calories. The book is most useful as a challenge to simplistic blame-and-willpower explanations of obesity.

01

The big ideas

  • Obesity is a chronic, biologically regulated condition—not a character flaw.
  • Insulin, food quality, sleep, stress, and meal timing can influence hunger and energy storage.
  • Frequent restriction followed by regain is common because the body adapts to weight loss.
02

What you can try

  • Reduce highly refined carbohydrates and ultra-processed foods.
  • Build meals around protein, fiber-rich plants, and minimally processed foods.
  • If appropriate, discuss a consistent eating window rather than grazing throughout the day.
03

Read critically

  • The book treats insulin as a more complete explanation of obesity than current evidence supports.
  • Fasting is not appropriate for everyone, including some people using diabetes medicines, pregnant patients, and those with eating-disorder histories.
  • Calories still matter even when hormones affect appetite and expenditure.
04

Questions to discuss

  • Would time-restricted eating be safe with my medicines and conditions?
  • What is a realistic first weight or waist goal for me?
  • Could sleep, stress, or a medication be affecting my weight?
