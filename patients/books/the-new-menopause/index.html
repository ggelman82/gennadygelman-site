Menopause · midlife health

The New Menopause

by Mary Claire Haver, MD

Mary Claire Haver introduces the changes associated with menopause and encourages women to seek informed, individualized care. This overview highlights the book’s themes; the practical care points below are general patient education.

01

The big ideas

  • Menopause can affect quality of life, including sleep and sexual comfort.
  • The book encourages informed conversations about symptoms and treatment choices.
02

What you can try

  • Write down your most bothersome symptoms and how they affect your day.
  • Ask about hormonal and nonhormonal options, including their benefits and risks.
03

Read critically

  • A book cannot determine whether a treatment is appropriate for your health history.
  • Do not start or change hormones or supplements without discussing them with your clinician.
04

Questions to discuss

  • Which options fit my symptoms and medical history?
  • How will we assess benefit and decide whether to adjust treatment?
