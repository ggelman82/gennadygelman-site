Perimenopause · hormonal transition

The New Perimenopause

by Mary Claire Haver, MD

Mary Claire Haver focuses on the transition before menopause, including changes in menstrual cycles, mood, sleep, and sexual health. The book offers tools for preparing for appointments and discussing symptoms. This overview highlights its themes; the practical care points below are general patient education.

01

The big ideas

  • The years before menopause can bring changes that disrupt daily life.
  • The book emphasizes recognizing symptoms and asking for a thoughtful evaluation.
02

What you can try

  • Keep a record of your periods, symptoms, and their impact to bring to an appointment.
  • Ask which treatment options address the symptoms that matter most to you.
03

Read critically

  • Do not assume every symptom is caused by perimenopause; other explanations may need evaluation.
  • Hormone levels fluctuate during the transition, so routine hormone testing is often unnecessary.
04

Questions to discuss

  • Does this pattern fit perimenopause, or should we look for another cause?
  • Would hormonal or nonhormonal treatment be a reasonable option for me?
