Where to get each vaccine

Some vaccines are not available in our office and should be given at a pharmacy. Any pharmacy works, and you usually do not need a prescription.

VaccineWhere
ShinglesPharmacy Always. We do not give it in the office.

04

If you have Medicare

Medicare splits vaccines between two parts, which is why some go to the pharmacy.

Part B (medical coverage)Flu, pneumococcal, COVID-19 and hepatitis B. You pay nothing if the office or pharmacy accepts Medicare.
Part D (drug coverage)Shingles, RSV, Tdap and other recommended vaccines. You pay nothing if you have Part D, or a Medicare Advantage plan that includes drug coverage. Without drug coverage you may have to pay for these yourself.
