Vaccine information

Tdap

Tetanus, diphtheria and whooping cough

What I want my patients to know about the shot that covers three diseases at once.

Who
Every adult
Doses
One Tdap as an adult, then Td or Tdap every 10 years
Pregnancy
One Tdap in every pregnancy, at 27 to 36 weeks
Cost
$0 with Medicare Part D

Key points

Had Tdap as an adult?If not, get one now.
A booster every 10 years.Td or Tdap both count.
Dirty wound and 5+ years since your last shot?Get a booster.
Pregnant?Tdap every pregnancy, at 27 to 36 weeks.
01

Three diseases, one shot.

  • Tetanus comes from bacteria in soil and dust that get in through a wound. It causes painful muscle stiffness, which is why it is called lockjaw.
  • Diphtheria is rare in the U.S. today because of vaccination. We intend to keep it that way.
  • Whooping cough (pertussis) is back. The U.S. had about 35,000 cases and 10 deaths in 2024, and nearly 28,000 cases and at least 13 deaths in 2025.

Whooping cough is most dangerous for babies too young to be fully vaccinated. In adults it often looks like a cough that will not quit, and an adult can spread it for weeks without knowing. That is often how it reaches a baby.

02

Like a car registration.

Tetanus protection needs renewing every 10 years, and sooner if you have had an accident.

  • Never had Tdap as an adult? Get one now, no matter when your last tetanus shot was.
  • Every 10 years: a booster of Td or Tdap.
  • A dirty or deep wound (a rusty nail, a dog bite, a puncture full of soil) and it has been 5 years or more? Get a booster.
  • Pregnant? One Tdap in every pregnancy, ideally early in weeks 27 to 36. Your antibodies cross to the baby and protect them in the first months, before they can be vaccinated.
  • About to meet a new grandchild? If you have never had Tdap, get it before the visit.

Adults 65 and older: Boostrix is the Tdap brand approved for your age, but any Tdap you are offered is fine.

03

What to expect.

A sore, red or swollen arm is common. Some people feel tired or headachy or run a mild fever for a day or two.
