Apps I recommend to patients

Useful tools, not more digital clutter.

Choose an app only when it solves a specific problem. You do not need to download everything on this page.

01
M

Your health record

MyChart

Message your care team, review results, manage appointments, and—when enabled by your health system—enter home blood pressure or glucose readings in Track My Health.

Some health systems use their own branded MyChart app.

Dr. Greger’s Daily Dozen

A simple checklist of foods and activities emphasized by NutritionFacts.org and the books How Not to Die and How Not to Diet.

Sleep · insomnia self-care

Insomnia Coach

A free VA app with a guided weekly training plan, sleep diary, and practical tools based on cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I).

Can be used on your own or alongside professional care; it does not replace evaluation or treatment for sleep problems.

Privacy matters

Health apps are not your medical record.

Before entering reproductive, symptom, medication, or mental-health information, review the app’s privacy settings and collect only what will genuinely help you. Do not rely on an app for emergencies or urgent medical advice.
