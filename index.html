About me

I'm Gennady Gelman, a family physician. I care about making primary care work better, for patients and for the people who deliver it. Along the way I build guides, tools and the occasional chatbot, and this site is where I share them.

I teach students, write Medicolalia, an essay series on where medical words come from, and celebrate kilodays.

I live in Providence, Rhode Island, with my lovely wife and our pitshund, Penny. Off the clock you'll find me looking up at the night sky, foraging, on a yoga mat, tinkering with something, or out on a long walk with Penny and an audiobook.
